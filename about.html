About Ndlovu Poultry Farm

Our Story

Ndlovu Poultry Farm started in the backyard of Mr Ndlovu in the rural and township areas of Mpumalanga. What began as a small operation selling live chickens for R65 each has grown into a respected local business.

Mrs Ndlovu's innovative idea of providing slaughtering and cleaning services transformed the business, offering customers added convenience and multiple service options.

Our Mission

To continue growing for generation to generation, creating lasting generational wealth for the Ndlovu family and serving our community with quality products.

Our Vision

To work with major supermarkets like Woolworths, Pick n Pay, and Checkers, making our products available to more South Africans.
